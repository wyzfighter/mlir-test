OVERVIEW: FIR modular optimizer driver

Available Dialects: acc, affine, arith, builtin, cf, complex, cuf, dlti, fir, fircg, func, gpu, hlfir, index, llvm, math, memref, mif, nvvm, omp, scf, vector
USAGE: fir-opt [options] <input file>
